# Local demo voice notes

Put approved presentation recordings in `frontend/public/voice-notes/`:

- `voice-note-01.mp3`
- `voice-note-02.mp3`

The Finvait page plays these clips as anonymous voice notes. Audio playback is local to the browser; the demo transcript, transaction JSON, and duplicate result are preset and are not derived from the recording. These audio files are Git-ignored so a personal recording is not committed accidentally.

Only use recordings you have permission to share. Files placed under `public` are served directly by the website and are not private.
